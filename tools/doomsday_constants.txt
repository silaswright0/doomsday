Doomsday 2026 industry constants
================================
Sources:
- Civilian factories: World Bank NV.IND.MANF.CD (manufacturing value added), not GDP
- Military factories: SIPRI Military Expenditure 2025, industrial slice only
- Population: UN World Population Prospects-style 2026 estimates
- Dockyards: commercial shipbuilding share (China/Korea/Japan) plus naval yards
- Infrastructure: mapped from development / logistics (1-5)

Formulas (edit these, then rerun generate_doomsday.py):
  CIV_PER_MVA_BILLION = 20          # 1 civ per $20B manufacturing value added
  CIV_MIN_MVA_BILLION = 2           # countries above this get at least 1 civ
  MIL_PROCUREMENT_SHARE = 0.35      # share of milex treated as factory-relevant
  MIL_PER_PROCUREMENT_BILLION = 10  # 1 mil per $10B of that slice
  WARTIME_MIL_MULT = 2.5            # Russia / Ukraine / Israel / Myanmar

Worked examples (see doomsday_countries.csv):
  China MVA ~4660 → ~233 civs
  USA   MVA ~2500 → ~125 civs
  Germany MVA ~844 → ~42 civs
  UK    MVA ~294 → ~15 civs

Building caps (Doomsday overrides):
  industrial_complex / arms_factory / dockyard state_max = 40
  NDefines.NBuildings.MAX_BUILDING_LEVELS = 40
  NDefines.NBuildings.MAX_SHARED_SLOTS = 50

Debt and GDP columns are stored for a later stability system.
They do not buy factories.
