Doomsday 2026 industry constants
================================
Sources (full citations: tools/data/SOURCES.md):
- Civilian factories: World Bank NV.IND.TOTL.CD (industry: mining, manufacturing, utilities, construction) plus NV.AGR.TOTL.CD (agriculture). Not GDP. Not manufacturing-only.
- Military factories: hand-mapped defense industrial base (`tools/data/dib_mils.json`), not SIPRI military expenditure. Only tags that own 2026 territory.
- Population: UN World Population Prospects 2024 medium variant, 1 July 2026
- Dockyards: Statbase 2025 merchant DWT (UNCTAD/Clarksons) plus Janes/IISS naval construction campuses. NavBase fleet tonnage is a cross-check, not the count.
- Infrastructure: GLocal GID-1 area-weighted VIIRS 2021 (`viirs`). Not World Bank LPI. Breaks 0.08/0.35/1.20/4.00.
- Renewable parks: IRENA Renewable Capacity Statistics (wind+solar+hydro+geo+bio, not nuclear)
- Steel/minerals: World Steel Association + USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries
- Energy/oil/coal: Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy

Formulas (edit these, then rerun allocate_state_stats.py then apply_state_stats.py):
  CIV_PER_GOODS_BILLION = 35        # 1 civ per $35B industry+agriculture value added
  CIV_MIN_GOODS_BILLION = 2         # countries above this get at least 1 civ
  CIV_SOFT_FLOOR_BILLION = 0.5      # $0.5–2B goods still 1 civ
  MVA_TO_INDUSTRY = 1.67            # when industry is missing, scale manufacturing
  DIB_MILS = tools/data/dib_mils.json  # campus-unbundle: 1 mil = serial plant or workshop; USA 45 CHI 36 SOV 26 world ~419; no wartime×2.5
  INF_IC = 2.5–4.0                   # 2026 soldier kit (was 0.43–0.69)
  GW_PER_RENEWABLE_PARK = 20        # 1 renewable_park per 20 GW nameplate
  DIB_DOCKS = tools/data/dib_docks.json  # merchant DWT (1 dock / 2M dwt, min 0.8M) + naval campuses; no leftover CSV
  NTL_BREAKS = 0.08, 0.35, 1.20, 4.00  # GLocal area-weighted VIIRS nW/cm2/sr -> HOI4 1-5
  SRV_PER_B = 250  # 1 services_building per $250B services VA
  DIB_FINANCE = tools/data/dib_finance.json  # GFCI 38 campuses
  DIB_REFINERIES = tools/data/dib_refineries.json  # ~400 kb/d CDU
  DIB_SILOS = tools/data/dib_fuel_silos.json  # SPR / tank-farm campuses
  DIB_GRID = tools/data/dib_grid.json  # ISO/TSO hubs, 1 per state

Worked examples (see doomsday_countries.csv after ingest):
  China industry+agri ~8248 → ~236 civs
  USA   industry+agri ~4394 → ~126 civs
  India industry+agri ~1639 → ~47 civs
  Venezuela uses OECD 2014 SNA shares on current GDP (WB series is zeroed)
  China 2258 GW renewables → ~113 parks (placed on generation provinces, cap 10/state)

Building caps (Doomsday overrides):
  industrial_complex / arms_factory / dockyard state_max = 40
  renewable_park state_max = 10
  NDefines.NBuildings.MAX_BUILDING_LEVELS = 40
  NDefines.NBuildings.MAX_SHARED_SLOTS = 50

Do NOT rerun generate_doomsday.py to apply these numbers.
Use tools/allocate_state_stats.py then tools/apply_state_stats.py.
That patches existing history/states files and leaves owners, cores, VPs,
and province buildings alone.

Debt and GDP columns are stored for a later stability system.
They do not buy factories.

Treasury rates (US$ billions / month, tools/calibrate_economy.py):
  Civ factory income          1.00 per civilian factory
  Finance income              0.20 × level × (local civs + services + 4)
  Services income             0.15 × level × (local civs + 2)
  State admin                 0.02 per million people + 1.20 per civilian factory
  Standard tax                0.02 per million people + 0.80 per civilian factory
  High tax                    2.00 × standard
  Default policy factor       1.78 (additive extras, not 1.1^6)
  Interest                    3% a year (0.03 / 12 each month)
  USA start yearly revenue    ~3694
  Pay Interest Groups         1% of yearly revenue (50 PP, once per month)
  Subsidize Business          0.5% of yearly revenue (−5% consumer goods, 90 days)
